Page shells

Every demo composes around exactly one of five canonical shells, all defined in _shell.css. Two product shells (slate), two public shells (paper), and one focus shell. Open any one for an annotated skeleton.

01Sidebar app shell

Left nav rail + topbar. For data-dense product surfaces with many top-level sections.

.app · analytics · billing · inbox
02Top-nav app shell

Horizontal nav bar, full-width centered content. For product surfaces with few sections.

.app-tn · settings · live-trading
03Marketing shell

Pill top-nav in a centered editorial column, closed by a footer. Warm paper surface.

.mkt + .topnav · pricing · status
04Docs shell

Sticky blurred bar over a three-column layout: section nav · prose · table of contents.

.docs-top · docs
05Focus shell

Slim brand bar + centered, nav-free body. Modifiers: --split · --ink.

.focus · signin · onboarding · not-found
